# Dimension Range Description

Reward function

When Component Value What it teaches
every step distance to pad +/− Drift toward the landing pad, not away from it
every step speed +/− Slow down; moving fast is penalized
every step tilt angle +/− Stay upright; tilted lander is penalized
every step leg ground contact +10 per leg Get both legs down and keep them there
every step main engine fuel −0.30 Don't hold the throttle wide open; coast when you can
every step side engine fuel −0.03 Side thrusters are cheap, but still cost something
episode end soft landing +100 Touched down gently with both legs on the pad
episode end crash / out of bounds −100 Body hit the ground or flew off the side

A clean landing scores around +200 to +280. A crash typically scores −100 to −400. "Solved" = rolling-100 average ≥ 200.
