## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### "The app won't load"
- Wait 30 seconds and refresh the page
- Try clearing your browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete / Cmd+Shift+Delete)
- Try a different browser

### "I see old data or the app looks broken"
- Hard refresh: Press Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac)
- Clear cache and reload

### "The app is slow"
- First load after inactivity takes 10-15 seconds (normal)
- Subsequent loads are faster
- Try using a wired internet connection if on WiFi

### Still having issues?
- Email your instructor
- Include: Which week app, what browser, and what error message you see
